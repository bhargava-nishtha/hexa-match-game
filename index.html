📐 HOW TO PLAY
  • The Core Goal: Tap or click on a 3D isometric block that is touching one or more blocks of the identical color to pop them from the grid.
  • Combo Scoring: The larger the group of matching colors you clear in a single selection, the massive your points multiplier will be!
  • The Gravity Shift: When blocks disappear, blocks above them instantly fall into place, and new tiles cascade from the top to rewrite the board.
  • Move Constraints: You have exactly 20 strategic moves to score as high as possible before the simulation ends.
  • Accessibility Users: Use your keyboard's Arrow Keys to maneuver the white target cursor grid, and tap Spacebar or Enter to select your match.
